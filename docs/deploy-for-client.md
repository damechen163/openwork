# Deploy for a client

This is the v0.1 delivery-guide skeleton. Do not use Phase 0 for a production client.

1. Interview stakeholders and identify business outcomes and prohibited actions.
2. Verify the server, domain, TLS, network, backup, and recovery requirements.
3. Inventory data sources, owners, retention, and group-level access rules.
4. Confirm the identity source, administrator, groups, and approval owners.
5. Select a model/API approach and define data-processing boundaries.
6. Install only on a supported host and retain the deployment report.
7. Review every pack’s license and permission diff before enabling it.
8. Run a limited pilot with audit review and leakage/approval tests.
9. Train employees on capabilities, citations, approvals, and escalation.
10. Sign off acceptance criteria and known limitations.
11. Test backup and restore before handoff.
12. Agree on upgrades, monitoring, incident response, and support windows.
13. Record the responsibility boundary between client, implementer, model provider, and upstreams.

A service quote may separate discovery, infrastructure, installation, pack customization,
training, acceptance, ongoing maintenance, and optional SLA. It must not guarantee income.
